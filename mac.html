Ovrdue Helper for Mac

Beta build 0.1.0 (52). Updates itself from here on. For people on the test list; please don't share the link yet.

Download for Mac (1.8 MB)

Needs macOS 15 or later, and the Ovrdue app on your iPhone signed into the same iCloud account. Signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

Setting up

  1. Open the download and drag Ovrdue Helper into Applications, then open it from there.
  2. A setup window walks you through two permissions: Full Disk Access (so it can read when you last messaged or called someone) and Contacts (so numbers turn into names). It never reads what was said.
  3. Run the first scan. A card appears on your iPhone with the people it thinks are your friends. Put each one in a circle, or tap Work to leave them out.

After that it sits in the menu bar, rescans every few hours, and keeps your iPhone's "last spoke" dates up to date through your own iCloud. How syncing stays private.

Something not right?

Email hello@ovrdue.app with what you saw. The menu bar panel shows what each source found, which is usually the first thing I'll ask about.
