Terms

Last updated 23rd October 2026. Plain English on purpose.

What Ovrdue is

Ovrdue is an iPhone app, with an optional Mac helper, that reminds you to keep in touch with the people you choose. It reads dates, never the content of your messages, and keeps your data on your devices and in your own iCloud. See the privacy page for the detail.

Free and Pro

The free app lets you keep up to five people and log catch-ups by hand. Ovrdue Pro adds unlimited people, the Mac helper keeping dates up to date, calendar meet-ups, the Instagram and Messenger import and the weekly round-up. Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-off Founder purchase while that's offered.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage and cancel them in your App Store account settings, not in the app. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, and Apple handles payment, receipts and refunds under its own terms.

What we ask of you

Use Ovrdue for your own personal relationships. Don't use it to track people who haven't agreed to be in contact with you, and don't try to work around the limits of the free version or the protections around other apps' data.

What you can expect from us

We'll keep the apps working with current versions of iOS and macOS as best we can. Some sources depend on how other companies store their data on your Mac; if one of them changes, that source may stop working until we update the helper. Ovrdue is provided as it is, and we're not liable for a missed reminder or a friendship that drifted anyway.
