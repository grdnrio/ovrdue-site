Support

Questions, problems and ideas all go to the same place.

Get in touch

Email hello@ovrdue.app. It's a small team, so you'll get a reply from a person, usually within a day or two.

If something's gone wrong in the iPhone app, go to Settings, then Help, then "Send a report". That bundles the app version, recent activity and any crash details into a text file you can share with us. It never includes names, numbers or messages. On the Mac, the helper's menu has "Report a problem…" which does the same thing.

Common questions

Where's my data? On your iPhone, your Mac if you use the helper, and in your own iCloud. Ovrdue has no server that holds your data, and nothing about your friends ever reaches us. The privacy page explains how the sync works.

Does Ovrdue read my messages? No. The Mac helper reads the dates of your last messages and calls so it can work out when you last spoke to someone. It never reads, stores or sends what was said.

My dates aren't updating. Check that the Mac helper is running (look for the rings icon in the menu bar), that it has Full Disk Access in System Settings, and that both your Mac and iPhone are signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on. The helper's menu shows the last scan and any problems it found.

How do I cancel Pro? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Ovrdue. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund? Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete everything? In the iPhone app, go to Settings and choose "Start over". That removes your friends and history from the app and from iCloud. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place, so use "Start over" first if you want a clean slate.

Mac helper

The helper is a free download from ovrdue.app/mac. It checks for updates on its own, and you can check by hand from its menu.
